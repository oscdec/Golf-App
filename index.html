Finding your number. Most tour players land between 21/7 and 27/9 at 30 fps. Try each preset for a few swings. Pick the one that feels unhurried at the top without making you wait for the third tone.

Frames to time. At 30 fps one frame is 33.3 ms, so 24/8 is a 0.80 s backswing and a 0.27 s downswing. The ratio stays the same across presets; only the overall speed changes.

Mute impact tone trains you to hear two cues and produce the strike on your own, which is closer to playing without the trainer.
