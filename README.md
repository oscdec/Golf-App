# Resonant Golf

A personal golf app for Android, installed from Chrome. It works offline at the range and on the course.

**Open it:** https://oscdec.github.io/Golf-App/

## Tabs
- **Tempo:** frame-based swing tempo trainer (30 fps, 3:1 and 2:1 presets, count-in, session timer). Keeps playing with the screen locked.
- **Rounds:** course and tees setup, blind hole-by-hole scoring, Tiger 6 mistakes, GIR+1, penalties, feel tags, best 3 shots, results and a handicap index estimate.
- **Insights:** handicap index and score trends, scoring by par type, and birdies/pars/bogeys/doubles per round, over any date range; backup and restore of all data.
- **Practice:** seven games (Safe Side, Fairway Finder, Range Round, Distance Ladder, Must-Make Exit, Putting Combine, Lag Putt Two Putt) with history and Beat Your Average. Routines are next.

Data stays on the phone in Chrome's storage for this app. Nothing is sent anywhere.
